Latency numbers every engineer should know

The whole point: memory is nanoseconds, disk is microseconds, and the network is milliseconds — a million times slower than a cache hit. HFT and low-latency engineering is the art of staying on the fast end of this table. Values are order-of-magnitude (Jeff Dean / Peter Norvig, updated).
